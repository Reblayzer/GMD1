# Roll-A-Ball

After going through the tutorial and learning the basics, I wanted to extend the Roll-A-Ball tutorial into a challenging parkour game.
The goal of this minigame is to jump on different objects to avoid getting killed by the enemy and collect small cubes to win.
Besides from that I focused on the user experience by creating the main menu before starting the game, the options menu that can be opened while playing by pressing the escape button and the ability to look around using the mouse.
